Concentration Spell

When you cast a spell that requires
Concentration, place that Spell Card here to
show that you are concentrating on it. You
can end Concentration at any time (no action
required). These factors break Concentration:

Another Concentration Spell. You lose
Concentration on a spell the moment you
start casting another spell that requires
Concentration.

Damage. If you take Damage, you must
succeed on a Constitution saving throw to
maintain Concentration. The DC equals 10
or half the damage taken (rounded down),
whichever is higher.

Incapacitated or Dead. Your Concentration
ends if you have the Incapacitated condition
or you die.

SPELL

SPELL ATTACK ROLLS: 20 + 4

SPELL SAVE DC: 12

Paladin Class Features

Lay On Hands

Place 5 Hit Point Tokens here to create healing
pool. As a Bonus Action, you touch a creature
giving it Hit Points from the available pool. You
may also expend 5 Hit Points from the pool to
remove the Poisoned condition.

Spellcasting

Remove 1 Power Token below to cast a level 1
spell from one of your Spell Cards.

PLAYING A PALADIN

Choose this class if you want to play a
holy warrior who wields divine power to
protects allies and smite foes.

Paladin Tips. In combat, stay on the
front line with your Longsword and shield.
Use Lay on Hands to restore your allies
when they fall.

Out of combat, your presence, and
conviction make you a strong leader
(Persuasion) and your keen intuition
helps discern truths (Insight).

Recommended Origin. If you need
help deciding your origin, the Soldier
Background Card and the Human Species
Card complement this class well.

Gaining a Level. When you advance to
level 2, flip this Class Board to the level 2
side and retrieve the additional com-
ponents listed in the “What You Need to
Play” section on that side. The adventure
states when this advancement occurs.

After a Short Rest:

  • Regain 7 Hit Point Tokens.

After a Long Rest:

  • Regain all Hit Point Tokens, Hit Point Pool Tokens,
    and all expended Power Tokens.
