Bonus Action: expend Channel Divinity to sense celestials, fiends, and undead within 60 feet for 10 minutes, and detect consecrated or desecrated places.

CHANNEL DIVINITY

Class Progression

3 Sacred Oath: choose a subclass; features at levels 3, 7, 15, and 20.

5 Extra Attack; Faithful Steed (Find Steed always prepared, one free cast per Long Rest).

6 Aura of Protection: you and nearby allies add your Charisma modifier to saves.

9 Abjure Foes. 10 Aura of Courage. 11 Radiant Strikes (+1d8).

14 Restoring Touch. 18 Aura expands to 30 feet.

After a Short Rest:

  • Channel Divinity: regain one expended use.

After a Long Rest:

  • Lay on Hands pool fully replenishes.
  • All expended spell slots are regained.
  • Divine Smite cast becomes available again.
  • Find Steed cast returns.
  • Channel Divinity: all expended uses regained.
  • Change 1 spell on your prepared spell list
  • Change Weapon Mastery properties.
