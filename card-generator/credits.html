Rules and equipment data

This work includes material taken from the System Reference Document 5.2 ("SRD 5.2") by Wizards of the Coast LLC and available at the official SRD website. The SRD 5.2 is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License.

The local spell and equipment libraries were retrieved from Open5e. Equipment is restricted to the srd-2024 source: 203 standard equipment entries and 760 magic-item variants. Retrieved October 25, 2026. Entries are normalized for card layout; weapon properties, mastery rules, range, armor requirements, and attunement text are combined into their cards. Generic magic entries in the equipment endpoint (Ioun Stone, giant-strength potions, healing potions, and spell scrolls) are marked as magical; families with multiple rarities use "Varies", and the basic Potion of Healing uses "Common". Magic-item descriptions retain their rules and bonuses; the compact weapon stats show the underlying weapon profile. Consult the description for magical modifiers. An unlisted magic-item price is displayed as "Not listed", not as a free item.

2024 Player's Handbook spell supplement

The separate PHB supplement contains original rules summaries for 51 non-SRD spells. These are not verbatim book text and are not represented as SRD or CC BY 4.0 material. Spell mechanics and base-class membership were checked against 5etools, using its 2024 Player's Handbook (XPHB) data, on October 27, 2026. Each entry links to its reference. Consult the handbook for authoritative rules.

The combined spell library contains all 391 spells in the 2024 PHB and covers all levels of its base spell lists for Bard, Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger, Sorcerer, Warlock, and Wizard. Subclass and feat grants, other books, and Artificer (not in the 2024 PHB) are outside this coverage check. Named spells already present under SRD aliases are not duplicated: for example, Tasha's Hideous Laughter uses Hideous Laughter and Mordenkainen's Sword uses Arcane Sword. Summoning summaries reference the required 2024 spirit stat blocks, which are not reproduced here. The app loads all spell data locally; it does not contact 5etools at runtime.

Hand-painted illustrations

Ravenmore, Fantasy Icon Pack 2.0, licensed under CC BY 3.0. Selected 512-pixel PNGs are bundled unmodified. These are representative illustrations, not official D&D artwork or exact depictions of every item variant.

Local artwork names map to the original pack: sword = upg_sword; dagger = dagger; axe = axe; hammer = hammer; spear = upg_spear; bow = bow; armor = armor; shield = shield; potion = potionRed; book = tome; staff = wand; pack = backpack; gem = gemBlue; tools = tools; scroll = scroll. Local filenames have the art- prefix.
