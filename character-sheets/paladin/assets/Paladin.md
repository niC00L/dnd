## Core Traits
**Primary Ability:** Strength and Charisma
**Hit Point Die:** D10 per Paladin level
**Hit Points at Level 1:** 10 + Con. modifier
**Hit Points per additional Paladin Level:** D10 + your Con. modifier, or, 6 + your Con. modifier
**Saving Throw Proficiencies:** Wisdom, Charisma
**Skill Proficiencies:** _Choose 2:_ Athletics, Insight, Intimidation, Medicine, Persuasion, or Religion.
**Weapon Proficiencies:** [Simple](https://5e.tools/items.html#blankhash,flsttype:simple%20weapon=1) and [Martial weapons](https://5e.tools/items.html#blankhash,flsttype:martial%20weapon=1)
**Armor Training:** [Light](https://5e.tools/items.html#blankhash,flsttype:light%20armor=1), [Medium](https://5e.tools/items.html#blankhash,flsttype:medium%20armor=1), and [Heavy armor](https://5e.tools/items.html#blankhash,flsttype:heavy%20armor=1) and [Shields](https://5e.tools/items.html#shield_xphb)
## Paladin Class Features
### Level 1
- **Lay on Hands:** Bonus-action touch healing
- **Weapon Mastery:** Pick two proficient weapon types for their mastery properties; change them on a Long Rest.
### Level 2
- **Fighting Style / Blessed Warrior:** Take a Fighting Style feat, or pick Blessed Warrior: two Cleric cantrips as paladin spells, swappable per level.
- **Paladin's Smite:** Divine Smite always prepared; cast it once without a slot until your next Long Rest.
### Level 3
- **Channel Divinity:** Two uses; +1 use at level 11. One use per Short Rest, all per Long Rest. Save DC = spell save DC. Effect: Divine Sense (bonus action, 10 min) detects celestials, fiends, undead within 60 ft and consecrated/desecrated sites.
- **Subclass:** Choose a subclass
### Level 4
- Ability Score Improvement feat or another qualifying feat.
### Level 5
- **Extra Attack:** Two attacks with the Attack action.
- **Faithful Steed:** Find Steed always prepared; free cast once per Long Rest.
### Level 6
- **Aura of Protection:** 10-ft aura; you and allies add your Cha modifier (min +1) to saves. Inactive while Incapacitated; only one such aura applies at a time.
### Level 8
- Ability Score Improvement feat or another qualifying feat.
### Level 9
- **Abjure Foes:** Spend Channel Divinity (Magic action) to frighten up to your Cha modifier targets within 60 ft (Wis save negates). Lasts 1 minute or until damaged; frightened target may do only one of move/action/bonus action.
### Level 10
- **Aura of Courage:** You and allies in the aura are immune to Frightened.
### Level 11
- **Radiant Strikes:** Melee/unarmed hits deal +1d8 radiant.
### Level 12
- Ability Score Improvement feat or another qualifying feat.
### Level 14
- **Restoring Touch:** Spend 5 Lay on Hands points per condition to remove Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Paralyzed, or Stunned.
### Level 16
- Ability Score Improvement feat or another qualifying feat.
### Level 18
- **Aura Expansion:** Aura expands to 30 ft.
### Level 19
- Epic Boon feat or another qualifying feat.

## Subclasses
Choose one oath at level 3. Oath spells are always prepared. Channel Divinity options change per oath. Level 7, 15, and 20 features are oath-specific. You cannot mix features from different oaths.
### **Oath of Devotion**
**Description:** The classic "knight in shining armor." They uphold justice, order, and truth. Devout servants of law and good.
**Spells:** *Alarm*, *Bane*, *Beacon of Hope*, *Compelled Duel*, *Create or Destroy Water*, *Crown of Faith*, *Detect Evil and Good*, *Find Steed*, *Lesser Restoration*, *Protection from Energy*, *Protection from Poison*, *Sanctuary*, *Silence*, *Warding Bond*, *Zone of Truth*.

**Features:**
*   **Sacred Weapon (L3):** Channel Divinity. **Bonus Action**. Add Charisma mod to attacks. Deal Radiant damage. Lasts 10 mins.
*   **Aura of Devotion (L7):** Passive Aura. Immunity to *Charmed* for you and allies.
*   **Smite of Protection (L15):** Passive Trigger. When casting *Divine Smite*, you and allies gain Half Cover until your next turn.
*   **Holy Nimbus (L20):** Class Feature. **Bonus Action**. Aura becomes sunlight. Enemies take radiant damage. Advantage on saves vs Fiends/Undead. Lasts 10 mins.
* 
### **Oath of Glory**
**Description:** Heroes destined for greatness. They train diligently to achieve glory through heroic deeds.
**Spells:** *Heroism*, *Longstrider*, *Raise Dead*, *Spike Growth*, *Thunderwave*, *Water Walk*, *Zoom*.

**Features:**
*   **Inspiring Smite (L3):** Channel Divinity. **Free Action** after *Divine Smite*. Give temp HP to allies within 30ft.
*   **Peerless Athlete (L3):** Channel Divinity. **Bonus Action**. Advantage on Athletics/Acrobatics. increased jump distance. Lasts 1 hour.
*   **Aura of Alacrity (L7):** Passive Aura. Your speed +10ft. Allies gain +10ft speed when entering aura.
*   **Glorious Defense (L15):** Class Feature. **Reaction**. When ally is hit, add Charisma mod to their AC. If they miss, you can attack back.
*   **Living Legend (L20):** Class Feature. **Bonus Action**. Advantage on Charisma checks. Reroll failed saves. Force missed weapon attacks to hit. Lasts 10 mins.

### **Oath of Redemption**
**Description:** Holy warriors who use violence only as a last resort. They believe anyone can be redeemed.
**Spells:** *Armor of Agathys*, *Bane*, *Calm Emotions*, *Crown of Majesty*, *Detect Evil and Good*, *Detect Magic*, *Hold Person*, *Lesser Restoration*, *Mirror Image*, *Protection from Energy*, *Silence*, *Spirit Guardians*, *Speak with Animals*, *Speak with Dead*, *Words of Healing*.

**Features:**
*   **Emissary of Peace (L3):** Channel Divinity. **Bonus Action**. +5 bonus to Persuasion checks for 10 mins.
*   **Rebuke the Violent (L3):** Channel Divinity. **Reaction**. When ally is damaged, attacker takes radiant damage equal to damage dealt.
*   **Aura of the Guardian (L7):** Class Feature. **Reaction**. Take damage meant for an ally within 10ft.
*   **Protective Spirit (L15):** Passive Trigger. Regain HP at end of turn if under half health in combat.
*   **Emissary of Redemption (L20):** Class Feature. Passive. Avatar of peace. Grants two defensive benefits against creatures that haven't attacked you.

### **Oath of the Ancients**
**Description:** Keepers of light and life. They cherish beauty and nature more than rigid honor codes.
**Spells:** *Charm Person*, *Crown of Majesty*, *Detect Magic*, *Ensnaring Strike*, *Enhance Ability*, *Lesser Restoration*, *Misty Step*, *Nystul’s Magic Aura*, *Plant Growth*, *Protection from Energy*, *Silence*, *Spider Climb*, *Speak with Animals*, *Stoneskin*, *Wall of Thorns*, *Wind Wall*.

**Features:**
*   **Nature’s Wrath (L3):** Channel Divinity. **Magic Action**. Vines restrain creatures within 15ft. Strength save or restrained for 1 min.
*   **Aura of Warding (L7):** Passive Aura. Resistance to Necrotic, Psychic, and Radiant damage for you and allies.
*   **Undying Sentinel (L15):** Class Feature. **Free Action**. Drop to 1 HP instead of 0. Regain HP. Once per Long Rest.
*   **Elder Champion (L20):** Class Feature. **Bonus Action**. Enemies have disadvantage on saves vs your spells. You regenerate 10 HP. Cast spells as Bonus Actions. Lasts 1 min.

### **Oath of the Crown**
**Description:** Guardians of civilization and law. They serve sovereigns, nations, or deities of rulership.
**Spells:** *Armor of Agathys*, *Command*, *Compelled Duel*, *Create or Destroy Water*, *Crown of Majesty*, *Detect Magic*, *Magic Weapon*, *Protection from Energy*, *Protection from Poison*, *Silence*, *Suggestion*, *Warding Bond*, *Zone of Truth*.

**Features:**
*   **Champion Challenge (L3):** Channel Divinity. **Bonus Action**. Enemies within 30ft cannot move more than 30ft from you. Wisdom save negates.
*   **Turn the Tide (L3):** Channel Divinity. **Bonus Action**. Heal injured allies within 30ft.
*   **Divine Allegiance (L7):** Class Feature. **Reaction**. Take damage meant for an ally within 5ft.
*   **Unyielding Spirit (L15):** Passive Trait. Advantage on saving throws against Paralyzed and Stunned conditions.
*   **Exalted Champion (L20):** Class Feature. **Action**. Inspire allies. Grants benefits for 1 hour. Once per Long Rest.

### **Oath of the Noble Genies**
**Description:** Paladins who revere elemental genies (Dao, Djinn, Efreeti, Marid). They draw power from the Elemental Planes.
**Spells:** *Conjure Elemental*, *Enlarge/Reduce*, *Gaseous Form*, *Gust of Wind*, *Misty Step*, *Water Breathing*, *Water Walk*.

**Features:**
*   **Elemental Smite (L3):** Channel Divinity. **Free Action** after *Divine Smite*. Apply genie effect (grapple, teleport, fire damage, or push).
*   **Genie’s Splendor (L3):** Passive Trait. Unarmored AC = 10 + Dex + Cha. Proficiency in one skill.
*   **Aura of Elemental Shielding (L7):** Passive Aura. Resistance to one chosen elemental damage type. Can change type each turn.
*   **Elemental Rebuke (L15):** Class Feature. **Reaction**. Halve damage taken. Attacker takes elemental damage if they fail Dex save.
*   **Noble Scion (L20):** Class Feature. **Bonus Action**. Fly speed 60ft. Make failed d20 tests succeed. Lasts 10 mins.

### **Oath of the Watchers**
**Description:** Vigilant protectors against extraplanar threats (aberrations, fiends, fey). They maintain spy networks.
**Spells:** *Augury*, *Detect Magic*, *Detect Thoughts*, *Hold Monster*, *Nystul’s Magic Aura*, *Protection from Energy*, *Rary’s Telepathic Bond*, *Scrying*, *See Invisibility*, *Telepathic Bond*.

**Features:**
*   **Watcher’s Will (L3):** Channel Divinity. **Action**. You and allies get advantage on Mental saves for 1 minute.
*   **Abjure the Extraplanar (L3):** Channel Divinity. **Action**. Turn Aberrations, Elementals, Fey, and Fiends. They flee or Dodge.
*   **Aura of the Sentinel (L7):** Passive Aura. You and allies get proficiency bonus to initiative rolls.
*   **Vigilant Rebuke (L15):** Class Feature. **Reaction**. Deal force damage when you or ally succeeds on a mental save.
*   **Mortal Bulwark (L20):** Class Feature. **Bonus Action**. Gain defensive benefits for 1 minute. Once per Long Rest.

### **Oathbreaker**
**Description:** Evil paladins who broke their oaths. They serve dark ambitions and evil powers.
**Spells:** *Animate Dead*, *Aura of Life*, *Aura of Vitality*, *Bestow Curse*, *Blight*, *Commune with Darkness*, *Conjure Minor Elementals*, *Contagion*, *Create or Destroy Water*, *Darkness*, *Detect Magic*, *Find Steed*, *Harm*, *Inflict Wounds*, *Magic Weapon*, *Phantasmal Killer*, *Protection from Energy*, *Scorching Ray*, *Speak with Dead*, *Spirit Guardians*, *Spike Growth*, *Stinking Cloud*, *Wall of Thorns*.

**Features:**
*   **Control Undead (L3):** Channel Divinity. **Action**. Command undead creature for 24 hours. Wisdom save negates.
*   **Dreadful Aspect (L3):** Channel Divinity. **Action**. Frighten enemies within 30ft. Wisdom save negates.
*   **Aura of Hate (L7):** Passive Aura. You and nearby Fiends/Undead add Charisma mod to melee damage.
*   **Supernatural Resistance (L15):** Passive Trait. Resistance to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.
*   **Dread Lord (L20):** Class Feature. **Action**. Create gloom aura. Shadows deal necrotic damage. Disadvantage on attacks against you.
